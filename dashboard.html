During the September rally Intents revenue rose to 3.2 times its August level and gas fees to 4.9 times, but active addresses fell to 87% of it, so revenue growth did not come with more active accounts.

Code: scenarios.py section 7 (post-hoc). Data: near_daily.csv. Index: 2026-08-07 to 2026-09-02 = 100. Blockworks active addresses have a definition break on 2026-04-02 (section 7); all three windows are after it.

Data and code: scenarios.py, near_daily.csv

At today's rate of new supply, buybacks only offset dilution if revenue grows about 9.4 times; a higher NEAR price makes it harder, not easier.

7. Is the April 2026 activity break real on chain? (E4, E5, pre-registered)

Around 2026-04-02 on-chain distinct signers fell 8.1% while Blockworks active addresses fell 89.4%, so the break is probably a Blockworks definition change (E4), and over the whole period the daily changes of the two agree too weakly to call the definitions consistent (E5, Spearman 0.479 against 0.60).
