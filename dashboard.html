Price Is Not Revenue: the 9.4x Question

Blockworks data, checked against source totals. Rebuild everything with python run_all.py. Research, not investment advice.

1. Where revenue comes from

Since September 2025 retained Intents revenue has replaced gas fees as NEAR's main revenue, and September and October 2026 were about four times the prior months.

Code: scenarios.py section 1. Data: near_daily.csv (protocol revenue, transaction fees).

2. Cheap or expensive depends on the definition

Counting only gas fees NEAR is the most expensive asset here; counting Intents revenue it sits below the peer median. Log scale, price to sales on 2026-11-01.

Code: analysis_confirmatory.py, H3 block. Data: near_daily.csv, peers_weekly.csv. Peers' revenue is reported as network-level only.

3. Does Intents revenue come with more on-chain usage?

During the September rally Intents revenue rose to 3.2 times its August level and gas fees to 4.9 times, but active addresses fell to 87% of it, so revenue growth did not come with more active accounts.

Code: scenarios.py section 7 (post-hoc). Data: near_daily.csv. Index: 2026-08-07 to 2026-09-02 = 100. Active addresses break on 2026-04-02 for an unknown reason.

4. Can revenue ever cover new supply? (sensitivity, not a forecast)

At today's rate of new supply, buybacks only offset dilution if revenue grows about 9.4 times; a higher NEAR price makes it harder, not easier.

Code: scenarios.py sections 3 to 5. Data: near_total_supply_weekly.csv, near_buybacks_daily.csv. Net new supply held at about 31.9M NEAR a year; buybacks at the median 72% of Intents revenue since 2026-04-23; revenue held in dollars.

5. Monitor: what would change the view

The 28-day average of retained Intents revenue is $67K a day; the pre-registered thresholds are $100K for 84 days (strengthens) and $30K for 56 days (weakens).

Code: build_dashboard.py. Thresholds: PREREGISTRATION.md, Amendment 7.
