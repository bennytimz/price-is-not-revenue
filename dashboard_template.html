Price Is Not Revenue: the 9.4x Question

Blockworks data, checked against source totals; sections 6 and 7 are public-source cross-checks (DefiLlama, NEAR BigQuery) pre-registered in Amendment 8. Rebuild everything with python run_all.py. Research, not investment advice.

1. Where revenue comes from

Since September 2025 retained Intents revenue has replaced gas fees as NEAR's main revenue, and September and October 2026 were about four times the prior months.

Code: scenarios.py section 1. Data: near_daily.csv (protocol revenue, transaction fees).

2. Cheap or expensive depends on the definition

Counting only gas fees NEAR is the most expensive asset here; counting Intents revenue it sits below the peer median. Log scale, price to sales on 2026-11-01.

Code: analysis_confirmatory.py, H3 block. Data: near_daily.csv, peers_weekly.csv. Peers' revenue is reported as network-level only.

3. Does Intents revenue come with more on-chain usage?

During the September rally Intents revenue rose to 3.2 times its August level and gas fees to 4.9 times, but active addresses fell to 87% of it, so revenue growth did not come with more active accounts.

Code: scenarios.py section 7 (post-hoc). Data: near_daily.csv. Index: 2026-08-07 to 2026-09-02 = 100. Blockworks active addresses have a definition break on 2026-04-02 (section 7); all three windows are after it.

4. Can revenue ever cover new supply? (sensitivity, not a forecast)

At today's rate of new supply, buybacks only offset dilution if revenue grows about 9.4 times; a higher NEAR price makes it harder, not easier.

Code: scenarios.py sections 3 to 5. Data: near_total_supply_weekly.csv, near_buybacks_daily.csv. Net new supply held at about 31.9M NEAR a year; buybacks at the median 72% of Intents revenue since 2026-04-23; revenue held in dollars.

5. Monitor: what would change the view

The 28-day average of retained Intents revenue is $67K a day; the pre-registered thresholds are $100K for 84 days (strengthens) and $30K for 56 days (weakens).

Code: build_dashboard.py. Thresholds: PREREGISTRATION.md, Amendment 7.

6. Did the revenue jump come from volume or take rate? (E2, pre-registered)

From the August window to the September–October rally, Intents volume rose 1.627 times but retained revenue rose 3.204 times because the take rate went from 2.61 to 5.13 basis points, so the pre-registered verdict is “mixed” (volume share 0.418).

Code: analysis_extension.py, E2. Data: raw_defillama_intents.json (DefiLlama free API, /summary/dexs/near-intents, pulled by pull_defillama_intents.py) and near_daily.csv. Mean daily values; window A 2026-08-07 to 2026-09-02 = 100, window B 2026-09-17 to 2026-10-15.

7. Is the April 2026 activity break real on chain? (E4, E5, pre-registered)

Around 2026-04-02 on-chain distinct signers fell 8.1% while Blockworks active addresses fell 89.4%, so the break is probably a Blockworks definition change (E4), and over the whole period the daily changes of the two agree too weakly to call the definitions consistent (E5, Spearman 0.479 against 0.60).

Code: analysis_extension.py, E4, E5 and X2. Query: pull_bigquery_daily_usage.sql (NEAR public BigQuery dataset, WHERE block_date >= '2024-01-01'). Data: raw_bigquery_daily_usage.csv, near_daily.csv. Each line is indexed to its own median over the 28 days before 2026-04-02 = 100; percentages are 28-day medians before and after.
